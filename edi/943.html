943 Receiving order (warehouse stock transfer shipment advice)

The 943 tells Capacity that stock is on its way to the warehouse. Each transaction set becomes one receiving order. When the stock arrives, Capacity receives it against that receiving order.

DirectionYou to Capacity
Version and GS01004010, AR
FolderIN in your folder
WhenEvery 10 minutes from 00:50 to 22:50; files that arrive later are picked up at 00:50, US Eastern
Answered byA 997 in the same run, then a 944 when the receiving order is closed

The Envelopes page describes the ISA, GS, GE and IEA segments that wrap the file.

Key rules​

  • W0602 is your receiving order (purchase order) number. Use a new value for each receiving order. It comes back in the 944 W1704.
  • W0604 is your shipment ID, for example the container number. It comes back in the 944 W1705.
  • Send G62 17 with the date the stock is expected at the warehouse.
  • Send W27 on every 943. Capacity records the SCAC and routing when W2701 is T.
  • Send one W04 loop for each item. Identify the item in W0404 and W0405 with its style (VN), UPC (UP) or SKU (SK).
  • Send N9 LI with your line number on each item if you want it back in the 944.
  • Send the lot number in W0414 LT and W0415 for lot-controlled stock.
  • Send your 832 for new items before the 943, so every item is known when the stock arrives.
  • Segments that X12 allows but are not listed here, such as N3, N4, PER and G61, are accepted and not used.

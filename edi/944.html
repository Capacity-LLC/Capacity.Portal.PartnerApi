944 Receiving confirmation (warehouse stock transfer receipt advice)

The 944 confirms what Capacity received against a receiving order that you sent as a 943. Capacity sends it once the receiving order is closed and the stock is available.

DirectionCapacity to you
Version and GS01004010, RE
FolderOUT in your folder
WhenAfter Capacity closes the receiving order
AnswersYour 943. You may answer the 944 with a 997

The Envelopes page describes the ISA, GS, GE and IEA segments that wrap the file.

Key rules​

  • Match the 944 to your 943 with W1704 (your 943 W0602). W1705 returns your 943 W0604 shipment ID when you sent one.
  • W1703 is Capacity's receipt number for the receiving order.
  • Each W07 loop reports one item: the quantity received in eaches, the 12-digit UPC in W0705 and your SKU or style in W0707.
  • A prepack is reported as one W07 loop for each component, with the component quantities.
  • N9 LI returns the line number from your 943 N9 LI.
  • W1401 is the total quantity received, the sum of every W0701.
  • Compare W0701 with the quantities in your 943 to find shortages and overages.

Some options, such as the second item ID in W0706 and W0707 and lot reporting, are agreed during onboarding.

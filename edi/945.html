945 Warehouse shipping advice

The 945 confirms what shipped for an order: when, with which carrier, with which tracking numbers and, if agreed, in which cartons. Capacity sends one 945 for each order that ships.

DirectionCapacity to you
Version and GS01004010, SW
FolderOUT in your folder
WhenEvery 30 minutes, for orders that shipped since the previous run. Each order is confirmed once
AnswersYour 940. You may answer the 945 with a 997

The Envelopes page describes the ISA, GS, GE and IEA segments that wrap the file.

Key rules​

  • Match the 945 to your order with W0602 (your 940 W0502). W0606 returns your 940 W0503 purchase order number, and W0607 your 940 W0505 pick ticket.
  • W0604 is the bill of lading number, or Capacity's shipment number when the shipment has no bill of lading.
  • Dates are US Eastern. G62 10 is the ship date and G62 17 the estimated delivery date.
  • W27 is sent when the carrier has a SCAC.

Carton detail​

During onboarding you choose one of two layouts. The choice can differ by retailer.

LayoutDetail area
With carton detailOne LX loop per carton. Each carries MAN (carton SSCC-18 and, for parcel shipments, the tracking number), PAL (carton weight), N9 TYP and one W12 loop per item in the carton.
Without carton detailOne LX loop per order line, each with one W12 loop. MAN, PAL and the carton N9 are not sent.

With carton detail, W1202 repeats the line's ordered quantity on every carton of that line, and W1203 is the quantity in the carton.
